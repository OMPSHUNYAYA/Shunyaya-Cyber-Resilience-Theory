# SCRT Theory at a Glance v2.18.0

## Central question

**When a system remains operational under compromise, what still justifies trusting that operation?**

SCRT separates two target-relative capacities:

`C_op` — operational capacity,

`C_cert` — independently certified capacity.

For target `k`,

`C_op >= k and C_cert < k`

is **silent assurance failure**: operation remains at target while the declared independent basis for trusting that operation has fallen below target.

## Core modeling objects

- **Operational route** — a declared defensive route with nonempty ancestry support.
- **Certified route** — a typed defensive/evidentiary route with nonempty physical ancestry support.
- **Ancestry** — the declared dependency support through which compromise can propagate.
- **Compromise state** — declared defensive and evidentiary compromise channels.
- **Hardening action** — an admitted action that adds or strengthens operational routes.
- **Certified response** — an admitted action that restores or strengthens certified support.
- **Resource claim** — a declared compatibility constraint on actions selected together.
- **Continuation contract** `C=(S,G,O)` — declared states, admitted future generators, and observer.

## Assurance-coherent hardening

Operational hardening can enlarge the set of compromise states that remain operational while certified assurance is below target.

SCRT defines **Assurance Compensation Cost** `ACI_k` as the minimum registered certified-action cost required to make the hardening assurance-coherent.

Under the declared finite semantics:

`stronger operational hardening -> ACI_k cannot decrease`

`larger certified-action library -> ACI_k cannot increase`

`higher certified-action costs -> ACI_k cannot decrease`

`stronger baseline certification -> ACI_k cannot increase`.

## Local interaction bounds

For additive hardening at target `k`:

`operational activation rank <= k`

and

`local certified response rank <= k`.

Thus each individual newly survivable scenario and each individual certified repair has a target-bounded witness.

## Global phase boundary

The local bounds lead to two global regimes.

### Conflict-free certified resources

When selected certified responses compose freely:

`universal assurance audit order = k`.

For fixed `k` on explicitly represented finite inputs, the assurance-audit decision is polynomial-time.

### Exclusive certified resources

When certified responses compete for exclusive resources:

`global assurance audit order is unbounded by k`.

This occurs already at `k=1`, while both local activation and local response witnesses remain singletons.

Under the same restricted regime, assurance compatibility is NP-complete.

## Continuation foundation

For a continuation contract `C=(S,G,O)`,

`X ~_C Y`

when every admissible finite continuation generated by `G` produces the same declared observation `O`.

SCRT separates:

`exact structural semantics -> finite-dimensional multiplicity-sensitive kernels`

`target-k semantics -> finite target-saturated quotient`

`campaign semantics -> minimal residual right congruence for the registered campaign observer`.

## Scope

SCRT classifies declared finite mathematical encodings. It does not establish that a deployed system has been encoded completely, that evidence is authentic, that declared independence is operationally correct, or that the admitted scenario/action grammar exhausts reality.

Universal statements are supported by written proofs under explicit hypotheses. Executable checks provide finite falsification, cross-implementation comparison, regression replay, and package-integrity evidence; they are not substitutes for universal proof.
