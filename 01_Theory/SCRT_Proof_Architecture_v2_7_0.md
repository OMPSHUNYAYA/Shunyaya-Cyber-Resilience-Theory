## Universal versus finite claims

Universal statements are proved from the declared finite mathematical semantics.

Executable checks serve as:

- finite falsification surfaces;
- counterexample searches;
- implementation crosschecks;
- proof-aligned regression tests;
- constructive witness replay.

They do not replace the written universal arguments.

## Constructive converse pattern

At each finite-state layer, inequality of canonical states is paired with a finite distinguishing continuation:

- typed exterior contexts for `BRK`;
- reconstruction probes for multiplicity kernels;
- obligation-targeted probes for `ORIK`;
- future resource or transformation contexts for action kernels;
- target-completion probes for target quotients;
- attack/recovery contexts for obstruction kernels;
- residual future campaigns for `CRK`.
