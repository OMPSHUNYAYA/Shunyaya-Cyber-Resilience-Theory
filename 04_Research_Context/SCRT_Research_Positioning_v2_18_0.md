# SCRT Research Positioning v2.18.0

Shunyaya Cyber Resilience Theory (SCRT) is an independently derived exact mathematical theory for declared finite cyber-resilience encodings. Its development proceeds from cyber-specific primitives, finite models, counterexamples, written proofs, and executable falsification. External research is used for later comparison and scope auditing rather than as mathematical proof input.

## Established structures used as tools

SCRT uses established mathematical and cybersecurity categories where appropriate, including:

- security assurance and evidence-based confidence;
- cyber resilience, redundancy, hardening, and recovery;
- finite-state behavioral equivalence and right congruences;
- partition refinement and finite residual minimization;
- target saturation and bounded witness structure;
- packing, covering, blocker, and hitting-set duality;
- fixed-point strategy reasoning;
- matching and resource-compatible selection;
- finite cost and Pareto optimization; and
- polynomial-time and NP-completeness analysis.

No generic method in this list is presented as an SCRT invention.

## SCRT-specific mathematical content

SCRT's contribution is the exact cyber-resilience semantics built from operational and independently certified route systems with declared ancestry, compromise channels, hardening, recovery, resources, scenarios, and continuation contracts.

Within that semantics, the theorem chain establishes:

`operational/certified separation`

`-> silent-assurance obstruction`

`-> assurance-coherent hardening`

`-> exact minimum certified compensation`

`-> target-bounded local activation and response`

`-> conflict-free global audit order k`

versus

`-> exclusive-resource global audit order unbounded by k`

`-> fixed-target polynomial / NP-complete computational phase boundary`.

The same theory also supplies contract-relative continuation and campaign classification, separating exact structural, target-relative, and campaign-residual semantics.

The significance of the phase result lies in this integrated SCRT structure rather than in claiming invention of the general mathematical tools used to analyze it.

## Comparison practice

Repository comparison material is category-level and non-bibliographic. It is intended to locate SCRT relative to established mathematical and cybersecurity structures without turning the repository into a literature survey.

No claim of historical priority is made, and the repository does not claim that no related formulation exists.
