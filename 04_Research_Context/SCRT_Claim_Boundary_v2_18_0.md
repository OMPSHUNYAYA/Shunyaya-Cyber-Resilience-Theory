# SCRT Claim Boundary v2.18.0

## Claimed within the declared mathematical semantics

- Exact typed operational/certified continuation classifications for the stated finite-interface models.
- Contract-relative contextual equivalence and canonical quotienting under explicitly declared future-operation and observer contracts.
- Finite-dimensional exact multiplicity representations at fixed interface width and sharp finite target-relative quotients under target-congruent semantics.
- Operational/certified divergence and role-polarized silent-assurance obstruction theorems.
- Exact round-resolved campaign values and minimal campaign residual quotients for finite registered campaign contracts.
- Operational-hardening silent-set monotonicity, activation characterization, evidence-only invariance under its stated baseline condition, and certified-hardening contraction.
- Exact assurance-coherence criterion and Assurance Compensation Cost `ACI_k` with its stated monotonicity laws.
- Target-bounded operational activation and local certified-response rank at most `k`, with sharpness.
- Under conflict-free certified compensation: universal audit order `k` and fixed-target polynomial decision on explicitly represented finite inputs.
- Under exclusive certified resources: no global audit-order bound determined only by `k`, already at `k=1`, while local ranks remain bounded; exact compatible-response selection; and NP-completeness of the stated compensation decision already at `k=1` under the specified restrictions.

## Modeling boundary

SCRT classifies admitted mathematical encodings. It does not infer whether a real architecture has been encoded completely. Dependency identification, evidence authenticity, implementation correctness, resource-conflict declarations, attacker-capability completeness, and operational validation remain separate obligations.

## Verification boundary

Package-integrity checks verify repository structure and cryptographic binding. Generated and historical executable checks provide finite falsification and implementation crosschecks. Universal mathematical claims are carried by written proofs under explicit hypotheses. No theorem in this package is proof-assistant mechanized.

## Not claimed

- Generic invention of security assurance, cyber resilience, redundancy, hardening, recovery, attack/defense games, contextual equivalence, saturation, blocker/packing methods, matching/resource allocation, Pareto optimization, or complexity reductions.
- That a finite registered scenario/action alphabet exhausts real adversarial possibilities.
- That target saturation preserves observers intentionally inspecting multiplicity above target.
- That static portfolio summaries preserve round timing under sequential recovery.
- That conflict-free resource results extend to exclusive-resource compensation.
- That the fixed-target polynomial result applies when the scenario universe is only implicitly represented and exponentially large.
- Formal proof-assistant mechanization.
- Historical priority.

## Research-positioning boundary

Repository comparison material is category-level and non-bibliographic. Established mathematical and cybersecurity structures are identified as comparison coordinates, while external research is not mathematical proof input to the SCRT derivation. No claim of historical priority is made.

## Security scope

The framework is defensive and structural. It does not specify exploit construction, malware development, credential theft, persistence, evasion, or unauthorized-access procedures.
